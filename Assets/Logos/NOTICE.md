# Provider logos

- `claude.svg`, `qwen.svg`, and `zai.svg` are from simple-icons 16.28.0.
- `muse.svg` is the Meta mark from simple-icons 16.28.0.
- `xiaomi.svg` is the Xiaomi mark from simple-icons 16.28.0 (used for the Xiaomi MiMo row).
- `openai.svg` and `codex.svg` use marks from `@lobehub/icons` (MIT).

The source projects and their licenses are documented in the project specification:
simple-icons (CC0) and lobe-icons (MIT).
